# Patina Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 21, 2026_

Patina is operated by **Ejmin Keshishi, trading as AppNovex** (appnovex.com), Yerevan, Republic of Armenia ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect and why. Contact: support@appnovex.com.

## What we collect
- **Account data:** your Apple user identifier, and the name/email Apple shares if you allow it (you can use "Hide My Email").
- **Items:** photos and optional notes you submit, and the AI appraisals we generate. Notes are only seen by our AI and never shown to other users.
- **Marketplace data:** offer requests, offers, predefined messages you choose, star ratings, blocks and reports.
- **Contact details you choose to share** (a phone number or email) after an offer is accepted. They are shown only to that counterpart, and only after they share theirs too.
- **Safety data:** moderation decisions, strikes and suspension status.
- **Purchase data:** subscription status from Apple. We never see your payment details.
- **Device data:** an Apple DeviceCheck token (to limit the free guest trial to one per device) and a push-notification token if you allow notifications.
- **Anonymous usage analytics** (TelemetryDeck): which screens and features are used, without identifying you.

We do **not** collect precise location or contacts. The only cross-app tracking is by Google AdMob for free users, and only if you allow it (see Advertising). Photo metadata (including GPS) is removed on your device before upload.

## How we use it
- To identify and value your items. Photos and notes are sent to OpenAI for analysis and safety moderation. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default.
- To share an item with the buyers **you** select. Buyers see your photos, the AI title and estimate, your asking price and your country. They do not see your name or notes.
- To keep the marketplace safe: automated screening of buyer profiles and photos, blocking unsafe actions, and AI review of reports. You can appeal automated decisions by email.
- To provide Pro features and prevent abuse.
- Legal basis (EU/UK): performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interest in safety and fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

## Where it's stored and for how long
Data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Photos are private. We keep your data until you delete it or your account. Moderation records may be kept for up to 2 years after account deletion to prevent repeat abuse, where the law allows.

## Sharing
We don't sell your data. We share it only with: OpenAI (AI processing), Supabase (hosting), Apple (sign-in, payments, DeviceCheck and notifications), TelemetryDeck (anonymous analytics), Google AdMob (ads for free users), the users you choose to interact with, and authorities when legally required.

## Advertising (free plan)
Free users see banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), IP address, approximate location and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent first through Google's certified consent message; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can refuse tracking in the iOS prompt or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; you will then see non-personalised ads. Pro members see no ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Your rights
You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Delete items in the app, or your whole account in **Settings → Delete account**. EU/UK residents may complain to their data-protection authority.

## Estimates disclaimer
Valuations are AI-generated guidance, not certified appraisals.

## Children
Patina is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

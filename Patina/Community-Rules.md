# Patina Community Rules

Patina is safe by design. Breaking these rules leads to warnings, suspensions or permanent bans, decided by our AI moderator.

**Always**
- Be honest about your item and its condition.
- Make only offers you intend to honour. Honour accepted deals, or withdraw politely.
- Meet in busy public places or use insured, tracked shipping.
- Pay and get paid through traceable methods. Hand over items only after payment has cleared.

**Never**
- Ask for or send "fees", deposits, gift cards, crypto, or overpayments with refunds.
- Ask for passwords, verification codes, bank logins or ID documents.
- Try to hide contact details, links or payment instructions in photos or profiles.
- Sell stolen, counterfeit, illegal or restricted items (e.g. ivory, human remains, looted antiquities, weapons where prohibited).
- Harass, threaten, discriminate against, or spam anyone.
- File false reports or create multiple accounts.

**Reporting:** open the deal → ••• → Report. The reported user is blocked for you immediately, and our AI moderator reviews the report straight away. Appeals: support@appnovex.com.
